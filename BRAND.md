# openElement brand assets

The canonical compact mark is the dark `<open/>` SVG in
`www/public/favicon.svg`. Website favicons, the generated starter mark, npm and
README references, and social artwork must use the same word mark and violet
accent. `open-favicon-inverted.svg` is the maintained light-surface variant.

Brand SVGs are distributed under the repository MIT license. Project forks may
reuse them with attribution, but should not imply endorsement by the upstream
maintainers. Product names and third-party marks remain the property of their
respective owners.
